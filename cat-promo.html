About Promo Makers

Promo Makers help CSC and VLE centres, shops and teachers advertise their services. Pick a platform, add a picture, write your points, and get a ready poster and message. The Pro version has a points dropdown with all SevaSphere tool names in English and Hindi, 22 point styles, 15 colours, video, PDF and HTML attachments, hashtags with a counter, and Group send to your saved groups. Everything runs in your browser and your groups are saved on your own device only.

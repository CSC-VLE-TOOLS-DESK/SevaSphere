About Smart Board

Digital whiteboard for teaching and meetings: pen, highlighter, shapes, text, sticky notes, pictures fitted with 4 pins, pages, recording and PDF save. Free for CSC centres, coaching classes and schools. Smart Board is a free online tool on SevaSphere, made for CSC and VLE centres, cyber cafes, students and job seekers.

  • Free to use, with no sign-up or login
  • Runs in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to our servers
  • Made for desktop and laptop, with touch and pen support on smart screens

- NEW TOOL: Smart Board (tools_smart_board.html, category Exam & Education). Digital whiteboard for desktop and laptop: pen, highlighter, eraser, line, arrow, box, circle, triangle, text, sticky notes, laser pointer, 12 colours plus custom colour, 8 backgrounds, many pages, undo and redo, save as PNG, PDF or project file, voice recording, full screen. Then move, resize, rotate, flip, brightness, contrast, colour, opacity, round corners, border, black and white, Auto enhance, copy, front and back.
